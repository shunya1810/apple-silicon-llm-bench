Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values compared to those with a "nominal" status within the same cyclical sequence. Because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), the "offline" record is the fourth in the sequence, while "nominal" is the first. Consequently, the sensor value for "offline" is typically 17.1 units lower than the preceding "nominal" value in the same cycle, but higher than the "nominal" value of the *next* cycle. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas Record 000000 (nominal) is 0.0 and Record 000005 (nominal) is 71.2.

Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying deterministic decay cycle. Nominal records appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, typically corresponding to higher sensor values within that specific decay segment. For instance, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10, while Record 000005 is nominal with a value of 71.2 in zone N95. In contrast, offline records occur three steps into the cycle, generally reflecting lower sensor values than the preceding nominal record in the same segment. For example, Record 000003 is offline with a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61, and Record 000008 is offline with a value of 54.1 in zone A56.

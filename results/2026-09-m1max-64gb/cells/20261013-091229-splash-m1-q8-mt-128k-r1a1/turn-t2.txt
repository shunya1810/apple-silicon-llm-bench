Records with an "offline" status generally exhibit higher sensor values compared to those with a "nominal" status, though both follow the same underlying sawtooth decay pattern. Because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), an offline record always precedes a calibrating record and follows a recovering one. Consequently, offline records often capture the upper-middle range of the sensor cycle, whereas nominal records appear at the very start of the cycle (highest values) or later in the sequence depending on the specific reset point.

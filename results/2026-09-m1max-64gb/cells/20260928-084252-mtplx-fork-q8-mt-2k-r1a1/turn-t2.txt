Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying linear decay of the dataset. Nominal records consistently appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, while offline records appear fourth in that same cycle. Consequently, nominal records generally have higher sensor values than the subsequent offline records within the same cycle, as the values decrease by 5.7 per step.

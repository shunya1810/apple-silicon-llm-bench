Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those marked "nominal" within the same descending cycle. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas the preceding nominal record, 000000, is at 0.0. This pattern persists across cycles: Record 000008 (offline) reads 54.1, while the subsequent nominal record, 000010, drops to 42.7. Similarly, Record 000013 (offline) is at 25.6, compared to Record 000015 (nominal) at 14.2. The status sequence dictates that "offline" always precedes "calibrating" and follows "recovering," placing it in the upper-middle range of the 5.7-unit decrement slope, while "nominal" starts each new cycle at the peak or near-peak values before the decline begins.

Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those marked "nominal" within the same descending cycle. This is because the status sequence (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) follows the sensor value decay; "offline" is the fourth state in the cycle, occurring after the value has dropped significantly from the cycle start, whereas "nominal" is the first state, occurring at the peak of the cycle.

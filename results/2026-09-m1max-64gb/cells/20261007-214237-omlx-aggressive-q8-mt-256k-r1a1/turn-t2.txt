Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values compared to those with a "nominal" status. This is because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), while the sensor value decays linearly. Consequently, "offline" records (the fourth in each cycle) always have higher values than "nominal" records (the first in the next cycle).

Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those marked "nominal." This is because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), while the sensor value decreases linearly by 5.7 units per record. Consequently, within any given 18-record cycle, the "nominal" record appears first (highest value), and the "offline" record appears fourth (lower value). For example, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 (the start of a new cycle after a reset), whereas Record 000003 is offline with a value of 82.6. Similarly, Record 000005 is nominal (71.2), while Record 000008 is offline (54.1). Finally, Record 000010 is nominal (42.7), contrasting with Record 000013, which is offline (25.6).

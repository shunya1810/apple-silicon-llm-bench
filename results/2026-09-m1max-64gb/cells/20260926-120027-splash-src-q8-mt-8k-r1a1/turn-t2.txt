Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those marked "nominal" within the same descending cycle. This is because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), meaning "offline" always occurs two steps after "nominal" in the downward trend. For instance, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, while Record 000003 (offline) is 82.6. Similarly, Record 000005 (nominal) is 71.2, whereas Record 000008 (offline) is 54.1. In the next cycle, Record 000010 (nominal) is 42.7, and Record 000013 (offline) is 25.6. Regarding zones, both statuses share the same alternating prefix pattern (A/N) and identical numeric suffixes for corresponding cycle positions.

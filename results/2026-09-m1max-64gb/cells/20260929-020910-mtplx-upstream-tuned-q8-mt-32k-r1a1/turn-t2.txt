Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying linear decay cycle. Nominal records appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, while offline records appear as the fourth item in that same cycle. Consequently, nominal records consistently have higher sensor values than the subsequent offline records within the same cycle group. For instance, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10, whereas Record 000003 is offline with a value of 82.6 in zone N61. However, because the sensor value resets after reaching near zero, the relationship flips in later cycles. Record 000015 is nominal with a value of 14.2 in zone N85, while Record 000018 is offline with a much higher value of 96.8 in zone A46.

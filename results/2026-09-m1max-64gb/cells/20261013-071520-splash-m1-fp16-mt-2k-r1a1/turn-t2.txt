Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those marked "nominal" within the same linear sequence. This is because the status cycle (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) means "offline" always occurs two steps after "nominal" in the 5-record cycle. Since sensor values decrease by 5.7 per step, an "offline" record will always have a value 11.4 units lower than the preceding "nominal" record in that specific cycle. For example, Record 000008 (offline) has a sensor value of 54.1, which is 11.4 lower than Record 000005 (nominal) at 71.2. Similarly, Record 000023 (offline) reads 68.3, while the preceding nominal Record 000020 reads 85.4. Finally, Record 000038 (offline) shows 82.5, compared to Record 000035 (nominal) at 99.6.

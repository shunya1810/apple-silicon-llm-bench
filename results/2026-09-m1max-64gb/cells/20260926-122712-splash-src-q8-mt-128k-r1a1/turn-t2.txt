Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those with a "nominal" status. This is because the status field cycles in a fixed sequence (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) while the sensor value decreases linearly. Consequently, "offline" records (the fourth in the cycle) retain higher values than "nominal" records (the first in the next cycle). For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas the subsequent Record 000005 (nominal) has dropped to 71.2. Similarly, Record 000008 (offline) shows 54.1, while Record 000010 (nominal) is at 42.7. Regarding zones, both statuses share the same alternating prefix pattern (A/N) and similar numeric ranges, but they occupy different positions in the zone sequence.

Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those with a "nominal" status. This is because the status field cycles through a fixed sequence (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) while the sensor value decreases linearly by 0.7 per record. Consequently, within any five-record cycle, the "offline" record (the fourth in the sequence) retains a higher value than the "nominal" record (the first in the next cycle). For example, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas the subsequent Record 000005 (nominal) has a lower value of 71.2. Similarly, Record 000008 (offline) reads 54.1, while Record 000010 (nominal) reads 42.7. Regarding zones, both statuses appear across the same alternating A and N zone prefixes, but the specific zone numbers differ based on the record index.

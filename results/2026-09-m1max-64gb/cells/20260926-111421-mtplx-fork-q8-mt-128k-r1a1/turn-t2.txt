Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values compared to those with a "nominal" status within the same decay cycle. This is because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), while the sensor value decreases linearly by 5.7 per record. Consequently, "offline" records (the 4th in the cycle) have higher values than "nominal" records (the 1st in the next cycle) when comparing adjacent cycles, or lower values if comparing within the same cycle start. However, looking at the absolute distribution, "offline" records appear at specific points in the decay. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, while Record 000000 (nominal) is 0.0. Conversely, Record 000008 (offline) is 54.1, while Record 000010 (nominal) is 42.7. In terms of zones, both statuses share the same alternating A/N prefix pattern and identical numeric suffixes for corresponding cycle positions, meaning zone assignment is independent of status.

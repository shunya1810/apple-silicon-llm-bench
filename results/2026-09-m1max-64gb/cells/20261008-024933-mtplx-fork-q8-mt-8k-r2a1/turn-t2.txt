Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying deterministic decay cycle. Nominal records appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, typically corresponding to higher sensor values within that specific decay segment, whereas offline records appear third in the cycle, reflecting lower values. For instance, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10, marking the start of the first cycle. In contrast, Record 000003 is offline with a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61. Similarly, Record 000005 is nominal with a value of 71.2 in zone N95, while Record 000008 is offline with a value of 54.1 in zone A56.

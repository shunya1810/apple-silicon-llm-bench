Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values compared to those with a "nominal" status. This is due to the fixed five-state cycle (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) interacting with the linearly decreasing sensor values. Since "offline" is the fourth state in the cycle and "nominal" is the first, offline records occur later in the downward trend of each 18-record cycle, resulting in higher readings. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas Record 000000 (nominal) is at 0.0. Similarly, Record 000008 (offline) reads 54.1, while the subsequent nominal Record 000010 reads 42.7. Finally, Record 000013 (offline) shows 25.6, compared to Record 000015 (nominal) at 14.2.

Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those with a "nominal" status, reflecting the underlying sawtooth decay pattern. Because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating), "offline" records always appear two steps after "nominal" records within each cycle. Since the sensor value decreases by 0.7 per step, an "offline" record is always 1.4 units higher than the preceding "nominal" record in the same cycle.

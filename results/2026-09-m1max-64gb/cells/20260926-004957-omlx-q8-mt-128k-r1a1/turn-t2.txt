Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status follow the same deterministic linear decay pattern for sensor values, meaning their magnitudes are not inherently different based on status alone. However, they occupy distinct positions within the five-record status cycle. "Nominal" records appear at the start of each cycle (indices 0, 5, 10, etc.), while "offline" records appear at the third position (indices 2, 7, 12, etc.). Consequently, within any given cycle, an "offline" record will have a lower sensor value than the preceding "nominal" record. For example, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, while Record 000002 (offline) has a value of 88.3, but this is because they are in different cycles. Comparing within the same cycle, Record 000005 (nominal, 71.2) is higher than Record 000007 (offline, 59.8).

Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status follow the same deterministic linear decay pattern for sensor values, meaning their magnitudes are not inherently different based on status alone. However, they occupy distinct positions within the five-record status cycle. "Nominal" records appear at the start of each cycle (indices 0, 5, 10, etc.), while "offline" records appear at the third position (indices 3, 8, 13, etc.). Consequently, within any given 5-record block, the "offline" record will always have a lower sensor value than the preceding "nominal" record. For example, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, while Record 000003 (offline) has a value of 82.6. Similarly, Record 000005 (nominal) is 71.2, whereas Record 000008 (offline) is 54.1.

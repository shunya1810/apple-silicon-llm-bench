Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit higher sensor values than those with a "nominal" status. This is because the status cycles every five records (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) while the sensor value decreases linearly by 0.7 per record. Consequently, within any given cycle, the "offline" record (the fourth in the sequence) retains a higher value than the "nominal" record (the first in the next cycle). For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, whereas the subsequent Record 000005 (nominal) has dropped to 71.2. Similarly, Record 000008 (offline) reads 54.1, while Record 000010 (nominal) reads 42.7. This pattern holds true across the dataset; for example, Record 000013 (offline) is at 25.6, while Record 000015 (nominal) is at 14.2.

Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying linear decay of the dataset. Nominal records consistently appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, while offline records appear as the fourth status in that same cycle. Consequently, nominal records generally have higher sensor values than the subsequent offline records within the same cycle, though this relationship resets as the sensor value wraps around.

Regarding zones, nominal records are associated with specific zone identifiers that alternate between 'A' and 'N' prefixes, as do offline records, but they occupy different positions in the zone numbering sequence. For instance, **Record 000000** is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10. In contrast, **Record 000003** is offline with a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61. Another example is **Record 000010**, which is nominal with a sensor value of 42.7 in zone A90, whereas **Record 000013** is offline with a sensor value of 25.6 in zone N5

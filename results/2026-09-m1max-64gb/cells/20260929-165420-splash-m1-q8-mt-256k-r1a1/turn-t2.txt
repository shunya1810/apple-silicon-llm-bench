Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit significantly higher sensor values than those with a "nominal" status. This is because the status field cycles through a fixed sequence (nominal, degraded, recovering, offline, calibrating) while the sensor value decays linearly. Consequently, "nominal" records appear at the peak of the decay cycle (high values, e.g., ~99-100), whereas "offline" records appear near the trough (low values, e.g., ~0-5). For instance, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 (the start of a new cycle), while Record 000003 is offline with a value of 82.6. Similarly, Record 000005 is nominal (71.2), while Record 000008 is offline (54.1). Regarding zones, both statuses share the same alternating pattern of 'A' and 'N' prefixes with incrementing numbers, meaning there is no distinct zone-based difference between the two statuses; the variation is purely temporal and value-based.

Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status exhibit distinct patterns in their sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying deterministic decay cycle. Nominal records appear at the start of each five-record status cycle, while offline records appear third in that cycle. Consequently, nominal records generally have higher sensor values than the immediately preceding degraded records but lower than the recovering records that follow them. In contrast, offline records consistently show lower sensor values than their nominal counterparts within the same cycle because they occur later in the decay sequence.

Regarding zones, both statuses share the alternating 'A' and 'N' prefix pattern, but their specific numeric suffixes differ based on their position in the global record sequence. For example, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10. Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61. Record 000005 (nominal) has a sensor value of 71.2 in zone N95.

   - **Ball Properties:**
     - Same radius.

     - Specific colors (20 hex codes provided).
     - Affected by gravity and friction.
     - Bounce off rotating walls realistically.
     - Collide with each other.
     - Impact bounce height constrained: not exceeding heptagon radius, but higher than ball radius.
     - Rotate with friction; numbers indicate spin.
   - **Heptagon Properties:**
     - Spinning around center at 360 degrees per 5 seconds (72 degrees/sec).
     - Large enough to contain all balls.
   - **Technical Constraints:**
     - No pygame.
     - Use only: tkinter, math, numpy, dataclasses, typing, sys.
     - Single Python file.
     - Implement collision detection/response manually.

2.  **Key Physics & Math Concepts:**
   - **Heptagon:** 7-sided polygon. Center at (cx, cy). Radius R (distance from center to vertices).
   - **Rotation:** Angle θ(t) = (360/5) * t degrees = 72 * t deg/s. Convert to radians for calculations.
   - **Balls:** Position (x, y), velocity (vx, vy), angular velocity (ω), radius r, mass m (assume equal), color, number.
   - **Gravity:** Constant downward acceleration g.
   - **Friction:** Air resistance/damping on linear velocity, and friction on angular velocity.
   - **Wall Collisions:** Heptagon walls are line segments. Need to check distance from ball center to each wall. If distance < r, reflect velocity relative to wall normal, account for wall rotation (tangential velocity of wall at contact point).
   - **Ball-Ball Collisions:** Check distance between centers < 2r. Resolve using elastic/inelastic collision formulas, conserve momentum, account for friction/spin transfer if needed (but maybe keep it simple: standard 2D elastic collision with restitution).
   - **Bounce Height Constraint:** "impact bounce height will not exceed the radius of the heptagon, but higher than ball radius." This likely means the restitution coefficient should be tuned so that after bouncing, the max height is between r and R. I'll use a restitution coefficient e (0 < e < 1) to control energy loss.
   - **Spin/Rotation:** Balls have angular velocity. When bouncing off walls or other balls, friction can transfer linear to angular velocity and vice versa. I'll implement a simple friction model: tangential impulse during collision changes angular velocity.

3.  **Implementation Plan (Tkinter Canvas):**

   - Heptagon center at (canvas_width/2, canvas_height/2).
   - Heptagon radius R chosen to fit 20 balls comfortably (e.g., R = 300, r = 15).
   - Animation loop using `canvas.after(delay, update)`.
   - Physics update step:
     - Update ball positions/velocities/angles.
     - Apply gravity, air friction, angular friction.
     - Check wall collisions (7 walls).
     - Check ball-ball collisions.
     - Update heptagon rotation angle.
     - Draw heptagon and balls.

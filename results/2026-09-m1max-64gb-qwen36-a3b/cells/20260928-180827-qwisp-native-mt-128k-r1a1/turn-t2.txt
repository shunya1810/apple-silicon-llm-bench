Comparing "offline" and "nominal" records reveals distinct patterns in both sensor values and zone assignments. "Nominal" records consistently occur at indices where the record number modulo 5 equals 0 (e.g., Record 000000, 000005). These records typically exhibit higher sensor values, often starting near 94.0 or 88.3 and decreasing gradually, or resetting to high values like 99.6. In contrast, "offline" records appear at indices where the modulo 5 equals 3 (e.g., Record 000003, 000008). These records generally show lower sensor values within their respective cycles, often dropping to the lower end of the 0–94 range before the next cycle resets.

Regarding zones, "nominal" records predominantly feature zones starting with 'A' (e.g., A10, A44, A78), while "offline" records are almost exclusively associated with zones starting with 'N' (e.g., N61, N56, N73).

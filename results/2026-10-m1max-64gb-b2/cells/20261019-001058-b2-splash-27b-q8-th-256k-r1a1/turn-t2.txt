=====
Both offline and nominal records appear at the same frequency (one per five-record status cycle), so each samples the same 18-record sawtooth at a fixed offset. Because the sensor decreases by 5.7 per record, a nominal and the offline record three slots later in the same group differ by roughly 17.1 in sensor value. However, because the sawtooth period (≈18) and status period (5) are not commensurate, the *absolute* values hit by each status are scattered across 0–99 with no single offset.

Zone coding is the sharper distinction. Nominal zones always carry a number divisible by 5 (A10, N95, A90 …) with the letter starting on **A** and alternating. Offline zones always carry a number ≡ 1 (mod 5) (N61, A56, N51 …) with the letter starting on **N** and alternating in the opposite phase.

Three records illustrate this:

- **Record 000000** – nominal, sensor 0.0, zone A10 (number mod 5 = 0, prefix A).
- **Record 000003** – offline, sensor 82.6, zone N61 (number mod 5 = 1, prefix N).
- **Record 000018** – offline, sensor 96.8, zone A46 (number mod 5 = 1, prefix A, second slot in the offline letter-alternation).

The zone-number congruence class is the invariant that cleanly separates the two statuses; sensor magnitude alone does not.

Each 18-record cycle begins near 0.0, jumps to a peak in the 94–99.6 range, then declines linearly by 5.7 per step before wrapping. **Three concrete observations**

1. **Deterministic sensor formula:** For any record *i*, the sensor value equals `peak − 5.7 × (i mod 18)`, where *peak* starts at 94.0 for the first cycle and shifts slightly each cycle, so the value at record 5,000 (≈ 48.4) is exactly reproducible without inspecting the data.
2. **Status is a pure index function:** `status = ["nominal","degraded","recovering","offline","calibrating"][i mod 5]`; a record's status never correlates with whether its sensor reading is high or low.

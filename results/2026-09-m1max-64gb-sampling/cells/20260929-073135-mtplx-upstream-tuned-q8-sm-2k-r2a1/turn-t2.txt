Records with a "nominal" status and those marked "offline" follow the same underlying systematic patterns but occupy different points in their respective cycles. Both sets exhibit the same linear downward trend in sensor values and the same alternating zone prefixes ('A' and 'N') with incrementing suffixes. The primary difference lies in their position within the five-state status cycle, which dictates their specific indices and, consequently, their exact sensor readings and zone assignments.

For instance, Record 000000 is nominal with a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10. In contrast, Record 000003 is offline, showing a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61. Further down the list, Record 000008 is offline with a sensor value of 54.1 in zone A56, whereas Record 000010 is nominal with a sensor value of 42.7 in zone A90. These examples illustrate that while offline and nominal records share the same structural logic for value decrement and zone labeling, their specific numerical and spatial identifiers are offset due to their different positions in the status sequence.

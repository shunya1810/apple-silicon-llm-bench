Records with "offline" status and those with "nominal" status exhibit identical structural behaviors but differ in their phase within the deterministic cycles. Both groups follow the same 20-record sawtooth pattern for sensor values and the 5-record status cycle, meaning an "offline" record and a "nominal" record are never adjacent; they are separated by two other status types. However, their absolute sensor values depend entirely on their position in the 20-record cycle, not their status. For example, Record 000008 is offline with a sensor value of 54.1 in zone A56. Record 000000 is nominal with a value of 0.0 in zone A10. Conversely, Record 000090 is nominal with a value of 85.2 in zone A10. This demonstrates that status does not correlate with sensor magnitude; high-value sensors can be nominal or offline. Zones also show no specific bias toward either status, as both types appear across all A and N zone sequences. The differences are purely positional within the synthetic loops.

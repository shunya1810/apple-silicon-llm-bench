Records with a "nominal" status generally exhibit sensor values that start the descending sawtooth cycle (e.g., Record 000000 has a sensor value of 0.0, while Record 000005 is 71.2). In contrast, "offline" records are fixed to a specific position within this cycle, consistently showing values that are three steps below the current cycle's peak start. For instance, Record 000003 has a sensor value of 82.6, and Record 000008 has 54.1.

Regarding zones, nominal and offline records share the same alternating prefix pattern (A or N) but differ in their specific numerical assignments. Nominal zones typically have lower numerical suffixes than offline zones at the same step in the sequence. For example, Record 000000 is in zone A10, whereas the subsequent offline Record 000003 is in zone N61. Similarly, Record 000005 (nominal, N95) is followed by Record 000008 (offline, A56). This indicates that while the status and sensor values are tied to their position in the 5-record cycle, the zone assignment is independent and strictly incremented, causing offline and nominal records to occupy distinct, non-overlapping zones at any given step.

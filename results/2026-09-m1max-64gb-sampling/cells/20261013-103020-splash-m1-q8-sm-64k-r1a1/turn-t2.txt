Records with an "offline" status and those with a "nominal" status are not distinguished by their zone designations, as both types appear across the same rotating set of zones (A10–N99). The primary difference lies in their sensor values relative to their position in the 35-record cycle. Because sensor values decrease linearly over time, "offline" records (which occur every 15th record) and "nominal" records (which occur every 5th record) occupy different points in this downward trend. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) shows a value of 82.6, while the preceding nominal record 000000 is 0.0. Similarly, Record 000008 (offline) is 54.1, whereas Record 000005 (nominal) is 71.2. Later in the sequence, Record 000013 (offline) is 25.6, compared to Record 000010 (nominal) at 42.7. Thus, while zones overlap, offline records typically feature lower sensor values than the most recent nominal records because they follow them in the decremental cycle.

Comparing records with "offline" and "nominal" statuses reveals distinct patterns in sensor values and zone assignments. The "nominal" status consistently appears when the sensor value is at the peak of the cyclical decay pattern or at specific low points, such as Record 000000 (sensor=0.0), Record 000005 (sensor=71.2), and Record 000010 (sensor=42.7). These records predominantly feature zones prefixed with either 'A' or 'N', but notably, the sensor values for nominal status do not follow a single monotonic trend relative to other statuses; they are simply the result of the 5.7 unit decrement. In contrast, "offline" status occurs at different points in the cycle, such as Record 000003 (sensor=82.6, zone=N61), Record 000008 (sensor=54.1, zone=A56), and Record 000013 (sensor=25.6, zone=N51). While both statuses share the same alternating 'A' and 'N' zone prefix logic, the specific numerical values differ because the status is locked to a fixed position in the 5-record cycle, whereas the sensor value is a continuous function of the record index. Consequently, "offline" records capture intermediate values in the decay sequence, whereas "nominal" records capture values spaced exactly five indices apart.

Records with an "offline" status consistently exhibit specific positional characteristics compared to "nominal" records. Because the status field cycles deterministically, "nominal" appears at the start of each 5-record status cycle, while "offline" appears at the third position in that same cycle. Consequently, for any given sensor value cycle, the "nominal" record will have a higher sensor value than the subsequent "offline" record. For instance, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, which is lower than Record 000003 (offline) at 82.6, because 000000 is the very start of the entire dataset where the sensor resets to zero. However, looking at subsequent cycles, such as Record 000005 (nominal, 71.2) and Record 000008 (offline, 54.1), the nominal value is significantly higher. Conversely, if comparing the very end of a sensor cycle, Record 000095 (nominal, 56.7) is higher than Record 000098 (offline, 39.6). Regarding zones, the zone assignment is independent of status and cycles through a list of 50 zones. Therefore, "offline" and "nominal" records in the same 5-record block will have different, sequentially assigned zones (e.g., Record 000000 is Zone A10, while Record 000003 is Zone N61).

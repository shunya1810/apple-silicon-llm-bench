Records with an "offline" status generally exhibit high sensor values, typically clustering between 80.0 and 96.0, whereas "nominal" records display a wide range of values, often starting low or appearing mid-cycle. This discrepancy arises because the status cycles independently of the absolute sensor value; "offline" occurs every fifth record, while "nominal" occurs at the start of each cycle. Regarding zones, both statuses utilize the alternating 'A' and 'N' prefix pattern, but their numeric suffixes do not follow an obvious correlation with the sensor magnitude.

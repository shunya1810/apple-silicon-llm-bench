Records with an "offline" status generally occur at indices where the index modulo 5 equals 3, while "nominal" records occur where the index modulo 5 equals 0. Consequently, these two groups do not compete for the same specific numerical ranges but appear in distinct, alternating sequences. However, due to the complex sawtooth nature of the sensor values, an "offline" record can have a significantly higher sensor reading than a subsequent "nominal" record. For instance, Record 000003 is offline with a sensor value of 82.6 in zone N61. In contrast, Record 000000 is nominal with a value of 0.0 in zone A10. Later in the dataset, this trend reverses relative to their position in the cycle; Record 000013 is offline with a low sensor value of 25.6 in zone N51, whereas the subsequent Record 000015 is nominal with a low value of 14.2 in zone N85. Notably, zone prefixes alternate strictly between A and N regardless of status, meaning "offline" and "nominal" statuses are distributed evenly across both zone types.

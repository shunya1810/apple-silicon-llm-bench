Records marked as "offline" and those marked as "nominal" exhibit identical distribution patterns in sensor values, indicating that the status field is determined by a fixed cyclical counter rather than by the magnitude of the reading. In both categories, sensor values oscillate from near 0.0 to approximately 99.9 in a repeating sawtooth wave. For instance, Record 000003 (offline) has a sensor value of 82.6, while Record 000000 (nominal) starts at 0.0. Later in the sequence, Record 000008 (offline) shows a value of 54.1, whereas Record 000005 (nominal) is 71.2. Both status types appear across the same range of zone identifiers, such as A10, N27, and A44, with no distinct clustering. Consequently, an "offline" status does not correspond to low or high sensor readings, nor does it restrict data to specific zones. The primary difference lies solely in the position within the five-step status cycle, not in the telemetry values or zone assignments themselves.

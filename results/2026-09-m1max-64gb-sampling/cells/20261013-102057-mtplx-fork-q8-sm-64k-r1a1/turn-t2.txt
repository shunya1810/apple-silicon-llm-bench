Records with `status=offline` and `status=nominal` exhibit distinct differences in sensor magnitude, zone labeling, and structural integrity, particularly as the dataset progresses. In the early, well-formatted section, `nominal` records often correspond to the highest or lowest points of the sensor cycle (e.g., Record 000000 at 0.0 and Record 000035 at 99.6), while `offline` records typically occupy the lower-to-middle range (e.g., Record 000003 at 82.6). Regarding zones, `nominal` entries in the initial block strictly use A-prefixes for even indices and N-prefixes for odd, whereas `offline` follows a separate rotation.

However, significant divergence appears in corrupted later sections. For instance, Record 001325 is `nominal` but uses a non-standard "offline" zone label, while Record 001328 is `offline` with a malformed "A86" zone. Finally, Record 002003 is `offline` with a clean N41 zone and 48.4 sensor value, contrasting with Record 002010’s `nominal` status, A70 zone, and 8.3 value. These examples highlight how formatting degradation obscures the clear early correlations between status, value, and zone.

The distribution of sensor values for "nominal" and "offline" records is nearly identical, as the status is strictly cyclical and independent of the sensor magnitude. Both statuses occur at every point in the 10-record sensor value cycle. For instance, **Record 000000** (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, while **Record 000008** (offline) has a value of 54.1. This shows that "offline" does not imply a low or zero sensor reading; it is simply a phase in the cycle. Similarly, **Record 000005** (nominal) registers 71.2, whereas the subsequent "offline" record, **Record 000008**, is higher at 54.1 (note: checking the cycle: 000008 is indeed offline). To better illustrate the independence, consider **Record 000010** (nominal) with 42.7 and **Record 000008** (offline) with 54.1. The sensor values for both statuses span the full range from 0.0 to 99.9 without correlation to the status type.

Regarding zones, both statuses appear across the same repeating set of zones (e.g., A10, N27, A44, etc.). For example, **Record 000000** (nominal) is in zone A10, and **Record 000008** (offline) is in zone A56. Since zones also cycle independently of the status, there is no specific zone associated with either status; they are distributed uniformly across the zone list regardless of whether the system is nominal or offline.

Records with an "offline" status and those marked "nominal" exhibit distinct patterns in both sensor values and zone assignments, though both follow the underlying linear decrease in sensor data. Nominal records appear at indices where the record number is a multiple of 5 (0, 5, 10, etc.), while offline records appear three steps after those (3, 8, 13, etc.). Consequently, offline records consistently have lower sensor values than the immediately preceding nominal records in their cycle. For example, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0 in zone A10, whereas the subsequent offline Record 000003 reads 82.6 in zone N61. Similarly, Record 000005 (nominal) is at 71.2 in zone N95, while Record 000008 (offline) drops to 54.1 in zone A56. This trend holds for later cycles, such as Record 000035 (nominal, 99.6, zone N65) versus Record 000038 (offline, 82.5, zone A26). Regarding zones, nominal and offline records alternate between the 'A' and 'N' prefix groups based on their position in the 18-record sequence, meaning no single prefix is exclusive to either status. The primary difference remains the offset in the descending sensor value sequence, with offline records always trailing nominal ones by three steps in the cyclic progression.

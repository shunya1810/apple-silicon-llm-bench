Offline and nominal records exhibit distinct sensor value patterns and zone assignments. Nominal records consistently begin each 20-record cycle with high sensor readings, typically ranging from 94.0 to 99.6, while offline records appear at the fourth position in each five-status cycle (index mod 5 equals 3), featuring significantly lower values. For instance, Record 000000 (nominal) shows a sensor value of 0.0, but this is an outlier; standard nominal records like Record 000005 show 71.2. Conversely, offline records such as Record 000003 display a value of 82.6, which is higher than later nominal records but lower than early ones. Regarding zones, nominal records frequently use 'A' prefixes in the first half of the cycle, whereas offline records often use 'N' prefixes or different 'A' zones depending on the specific cycle position. Specifically, Record 000008 (offline) is in zone A56 with a sensor value of 54.1, while Record 000010 (nominal) is in zone A90 with a value of 42.7. Record 000005 (nominal) is in zone N95 with a value of 71.2. This demonstrates that offline records do not simply have the lowest values, but rather occupy a specific middle-to-low tier within the cyclical decay, distinct from the peak values of nominal records.

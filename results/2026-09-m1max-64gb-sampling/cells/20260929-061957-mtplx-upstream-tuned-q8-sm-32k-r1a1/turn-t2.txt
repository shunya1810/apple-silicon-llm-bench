The "nominal" and "offline" statuses do not correlate with specific sensor value ranges; instead, both statuses appear across the full spectrum of sensor readings (0.0 to 99.6+) due to the cyclical nature of the data. For instance, Record 000000 (nominal) has a sensor value of 0.0, while Record 000003 (offline) has a value of 82.6. Conversely, Record 000018 (offline) records a high value of 96.8, whereas Record 000020 (nominal) is at 85.4. This indicates that system status is determined by the sequential cycle (every 5th record) rather than the magnitude of the sensor reading.

Regarding zones, there is no distinct segregation. Both statuses are distributed across the same set of zones (A and N prefixes with varying numbers). For example, Record 000000 (nominal) is in zone A10, while Record 000018 (offline) is in zone A46. Similarly, Record 000003 (offline) is in N61, and Record 000005 (nominal) is in N95. The zone assignments follow a repeating pattern tied to the record index modulo the zone cycle, meaning the same zones will eventually host both nominal and offline records in different cycles. Thus, neither sensor values nor zones specifically define the status category.

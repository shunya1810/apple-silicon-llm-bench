Records with "offline" status generally occur later in the 18-record cycle than those marked "nominal," resulting in lower sensor values for the same cycle iteration. Specifically, "nominal" status appears at record indices ending in 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, etc., while "offline" appears at indices ending in 3, 8, 13, 18, etc. Because the sensor value decreases by 5.7 per step, "offline" records in a given cycle have sensor values approximately 3 steps lower than the preceding "nominal" record. For instance, Record 000005 (nominal) has a sensor value of 71.2, whereas Record 000008 (offline) is lower at 54.1. Similarly, Record 000010 (nominal) reads 42.7, while Record 000013 (offline) reads 25.6. The zones also differ structurally: "nominal" records often reside in zones with even or odd digits depending on the cycle, but the key distinction is the consistent numerical drop. For example, Record 000015 (nominal) has a sensor value of 14.2, while the subsequent cycle’s first "offline" record, Record 000018, jumps back up to 96.8, breaking the downward trend due to the cycle reset.
